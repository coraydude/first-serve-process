About First Serve Process Solutions

Experience Behind the Service.Technology Behind the Process.

At First Serve Process Solutions, we believe process serving should be more than simply delivering documents. It should be professional, responsive, organized, and easy for our clients to manage. With more than 20 years of experience working in the legal field, First Serve understands firsthand how important deadlines, accuracy, communication, confidentiality, and follow through are to attorneys and their clients.

That experience is what inspired First Serve.
A different approach

Built Around the Way Legal Professionals Work

Rather than requiring attorneys and law firms to search for and manage individual process servers in different locations, First Serve serves as the central point of contact for coordinating service throughout Florida.

Florida Wide Coordination

One company. One point of contact. One place to monitor your assignments.

Your case doesn't always stay in one county, and neither should your process serving options.

First Serve is designed to coordinate process serving throughout Florida through a network of process serving professionals. Whether service is needed locally or across the state, our goal is to make the process simple for you.
