About First Serve Process Solutions

Experience Behind the Service. Technology Behind the Process.

Our Story

Experience Behind the Service

At First Serve Process Solutions, we believe process serving should be more than simply delivering documents. It should be professional, responsive, organized, and easy for our clients to manage.

With more than 20 years of experience working in the legal field, First Serve understands firsthand how important deadlines, accuracy, communication, confidentiality, and follow through are to attorneys and their clients.

That experience is what inspired First Serve.

Built Around the Way Legal Professionals Work

First Serve was created with a different approach to process serving.

Rather than requiring attorneys and law firms to search for and manage individual process servers in different locations, First Serve serves as the central point of contact for coordinating service throughout Florida.

When you send an assignment to First Serve, we coordinate the service with a process serving professional in the appropriate service area and manage the assignment through completion.

Our goal is simple

You focus on your case.
We keep the service moving.

Florida Wide Coordination

Your case doesn't always stay in one county, and neither should your process serving options.

First Serve is designed to coordinate process serving throughout Florida through a network of process serving professionals. Whether service is needed locally or across the state, our goal is to make the process simple for you.

You have one company, one point of contact, and one place to monitor your assignments.

Technology That Keeps You Connected

We know that waiting for updates can be frustrating.

That's why First Serve uses technology to make the service process more transparent and convenient. Through our client portal, clients can track their assignments, view available service updates, and access applicable documents without having to constantly call or email for a status update.

No wondering. No chasing updates. Just log in and see where your service stands.
